# ScoresMac

Live scores for 22 sports in your Mac's menu bar.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/BowlPulp/scoresmac-releases/releases/latest/download/ScoresMac.dmg)** · [scoresmac.com](https://scoresmac.com) · [Changelog](https://scoresmac.com/changelog)

> ScoresMac is in beta. Thanks for trying it early. If something looks off, email [support@scoresmac.com](mailto:support@scoresmac.com).

## What it does

- Cricket, football, NBA, NFL, F1, tennis, golf, UFC, chess, CS2, Valorant and more, next to your clock
- Pin a match and its live score stays in the menu bar; click it for the full card
- Favourite your teams so they show up first, and get alerts when their matches start and finish
- **ScoresMac Pro** puts the score around your MacBook's notch, with goals, wickets and winners playing right there. One-time purchase, for one Mac.

The menu bar app is free.

## Install

1. Download `ScoresMac.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/BowlPulp/scoresmac-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **ScoresMac** into **Applications**.
3. Open ScoresMac from Applications. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

## Updating

Download the latest release and replace the app in Applications. Your settings, pins and Pro license stay as they are.
